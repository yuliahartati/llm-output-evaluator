Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

Examples include language translation, image recognition, recommendation systems, and autonomous vehicles.

Modern AI often relies on machine learning, where models learn patterns from data rather than following explicitly programmed rules.

AI has applications across healthcare, finance, education, manufacturing, and scientific research.

Despite its benefits, AI also presents challenges such as bias, privacy concerns, and the need for responsible governance.
